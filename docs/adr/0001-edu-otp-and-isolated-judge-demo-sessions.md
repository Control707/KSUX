# 6-digit KSU `.edu` email OTP and two-way isolated `Judge Demo Mode` sessions

KSUX authenticates verified `Students` exclusively via 6-digit numeric email OTPs sent over Gmail SMTP to canonicalized `@students.kennesaw.edu` and `@kennesaw.edu` addresses, while serving hackathon evaluators through per-session isolated `Judge Demo Mode` workspaces (`demoSessionId`) rather than Magic Links, KSU Entra OAuth, or shared global demo rows. KSU Microsoft Defender Safe Links pre-fetches and invalidates clickable Magic Links, KSU's Entra tenant blocks unverified external OAuth apps without university IT consent, and paid custom-domain email/auth tiers violate the $0 budget. Scoping every `Judge Demo Mode` mutation and `Scrappy` retrieval query to `demoSessionId` (layered over live synced KSU institutional feeds and curated seed data) lets concurrent evaluators test real-time multi-tab WebSockets across three switchable `Student` personas without colliding with one another or exposing real verified `.edu` `Student` content to unauthenticated visitors.
