# `workspaceKey`-partitioned Convex schema, immutable `"ksu"` catalogs, 2-index `courseSections`, and per-tab `Judge Demo Mode` personas

KSUX leads every `Student`-authored Convex table, B-tree index, full-text `.searchIndex`, and selective 768-dim `.vectorIndex` (`posts`, `listings`, `events`, `clubs`) with a non-nullable `workspaceKey` (`"prod"` for verified `.edu` `Students`, `"demo:<demoSessionId>"` for `Judge Demo Mode`, and `"ksu"` for shared institutional catalogs), clones the ~50 boot-relative persona/social/chat/listing/pulse fixture rows directly into `"demo:<demoSessionId>"` on provision or reset while keeping `"ksu"` rows strictly read-only to demo mutations, keys `Club` and `Course Hub` `Group Chats` lazily on `chats` by `(workspaceKey, parentType, parentId)`, caps the ~`14,600` dual-term Banner 9 `courseSections` rows at two B-tree indexes (`by_term_crn`, `by_hub_term`), and stores `Judge Demo Mode` `activePersonaHandle` per browser tab in `sessionStorage` rather than on `demoSessions` or `localStorage`. Sharing global demo seed rows with copy-on-write overlays or mutating `"ksu"` counters/rosters from demo sessions risks cross-evaluator collisions and leaks into verified `Student` views, indexing `14.6k` `courseSections` heavily multiplies full-table index copies against Convex Free's `0.5 GB` DB ceiling, Convex `.withSearchIndex` lacks `OR` filters across `"ksu"` and `workspaceKey`, and storing active demo personas in DB rows or `localStorage` overwrites Tab 1 when Tab 2 switches personas during 2-tab live WebSocket evaluation. Partitioning by `workspaceKey` (~`40 MB` total DB + index footprint, `<30 MB` vector storage), pairing dual bounded `"ksu"` + `viewer.workspaceKey` text-search reads with `q.or(...)` vector filters, driving `shellSummary` off monotonic `chats.messageSeq` / `chatMembers.lastReadSeq` cursors (`0` `chatMessages` scans), and opening `?demo=<shortCode>&persona=<counterparty>` tabs in isolated `sessionStorage` guarantees leak-proof multi-tab demos and sub-`10%` Convex Free storage utilization.
