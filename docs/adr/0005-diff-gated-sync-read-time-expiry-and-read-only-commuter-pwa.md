# Diff-gated V8 KSU syncs, read-time expiry, targeted VAPID push, and read-only commuter PWA scope

KSUX splits external KSU ingestion into payload-hash-gated V8 `convex/crons.ts` jobs (operating-hours `2m` Passio GO snapshots, `30m` Localist + Engage syndication `Events` with selective embeddings, daily `hours-locations.php` + twice-daily Nutrislice `Dining Venues`), single-flight `5m` stale-while-revalidate LibCal `Today + Tomorrow` 48-bit slot masks (`30m` daytime warm cron), and deploy-seeded `Clubs`, `Parking Lots`, and Banner 9 `Course Hubs` paired with lazy `7d` Simple Syllabus summaries (`500`-row LRU), while enforcing sub-hour/session expiries purely at read/validation time via `expiresAt` (`0` status-flip crons, backed by one `:42` hourly `≤200`-row cursor-batched `demoSessionId` + `UTApi.deleteFiles` purge), scoping opt-in self-hosted VAPID Web Push strictly to handle-only, `60s`-throttled `Direct Messages`, `Deal Chats`, and `@scrappy` replies (suppressing `Group Chats`, `Spot Opening` fan-out, and `Judge Demo Mode` push registration), and limiting offline PWA support to precached app-shell/`/public/data/` GeoJSON campus maps plus a read-only `ksux_commuter_cache` schedule snapshot (`0` offline mutation queues, `0` offline `chatMessages`). Uniform high-frequency crons across LibCal (`robots.txt` `Crawl-delay: 10`), Engage (`/engage/api/` disallowed), and `14k` Banner 9 sections or per-minute DB expiry flips and `"use node"` broadcast pushes would trip upstream rate blocks and exhaust Convex Free's `1M` monthly calls, `1 GB` DB I/O, and `20 GB-hr` Action compute, while offline write queues, API/vector-tile Service Worker interception, or `Judge Demo Mode` OS push bindings risk reconnect conflicts, Safari storage eviction, shared-device chat leaks, and cross-wired evaluator personas. Guarding every upstream sync with `8s` timeouts, `3`-strike exponential backoff, and `>80%`-drop empty-payload aborts keeps live campus state under `3%` of Convex Free quotas without ever overwriting valid cached KSU data during university maintenance.
