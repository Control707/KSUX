# KSUX

The unified campus social network and daily-life companion for Kennesaw State University across both the Kennesaw and Marietta campuses.

## Language

### Identity & Campus

**Student**:
A verified Kennesaw State University community member authenticated via a `@students.kennesaw.edu` or `@kennesaw.edu` email address.
_Avoid_: User, member, account

**Campus**:
One of Kennesaw State University's two physical campuses — `Kennesaw` or `Marietta` — used to scope local feeds, parking, study spaces, dining, transit, and meetups.
_Avoid_: Site, branch, location

**Judge Demo Mode**:
A one-click isolated evaluation mode with switchable pre-seeded Student personas that lets hackathon evaluators use every interactive feature of KSUX without a `.edu` email inbox.
_Avoid_: Guest mode, anonymous mode, sandbox

### Social, Communities & Messaging

**Post**:
A short campus update, media share, reply, or repost published to the Feed.
_Avoid_: Tweet, status, wall post

**Feed**:
The real-time stream of campus Posts, filterable by Campus, following graph, or trending topic.
_Avoid_: Timeline, wall

**Direct Message (DM)**:
A private one-to-one conversation between two Students.
_Avoid_: Whisper, private thread, inbox mail

**Group Chat**:
A multi-student conversation for friends, dorm floors, study groups, Clubs, or Course Hubs.
_Avoid_: Channel, server, chatroom

**Club**:
A student organization at KSU with a directory profile, Events, and an associated Group Chat.
_Avoid_: RSO, guild, portal

**Course Hub**:
A shared community space for Students taking the same KSU course to discuss topics, form study groups, and coordinate Study Sessions.
_Avoid_: Class shell, LMS page, D2L room

### Marketplace

**Listing**:
An item, textbook, sublease, or service posted by a Student for peer-to-peer sale, trade, or free pickup on campus.
_Avoid_: Product, SKU, classified ad

**Deal Chat**:
An isolated Marketplace conversation per prospective buyer on a specific Listing, expandable to invited co-buyers or co-sellers to negotiate terms and coordinate handoff at a Meetup Spot.
_Avoid_: Checkout, order ticket, inquiry email, shared listing room

**Meetup Spot**:
A designated public, well-lit location on the Kennesaw or Marietta Campus selected inside a Deal Chat for an in-person Listing handoff.
_Avoid_: Shipping address, drop zone

### Campus Daily Life

**Parking Lot**:
A named KSU parking deck or surface lot tied to a Campus and permit classification.
_Avoid_: Garage, parking zone

**Parking Report**:
A time-stamped Student update reporting current fullness or an about-to-open space in a Parking Lot.
_Avoid_: Sensor ping, lot reservation

**Line Check**:
A time-stamped Student update on the current wait at a Dining Venue.
_Avoid_: Queue ping, wait report

**Study Room**:
A library or campus building space available for individual or group study on either Campus.
_Avoid_: Classroom, office

**Study Session**:
A scheduled or live Student study gathering linked to a Study Room, time window, and optional Course Hub.
_Avoid_: Appointment, class period

**Event**:
A scheduled campus gathering hosted by KSU, a Club, or a Student, open for discovery and RSVP.
_Avoid_: Calendar item, activity

**Shuttle Route**:
A Big Owl Bus (BOB) transit route connecting stops within or between the Kennesaw and Marietta Campuses.
_Avoid_: Bus line, commute track

**Shuttle Notice**:
A detour, delay, or service change published by the shuttle operator for one or more Shuttle Routes.
_Avoid_: Alert, service alert

**Dining Venue**:
A KSU dining hall (`The Commons` or `Stingers`) or campus retail eatery on the Kennesaw or Marietta Campus.
_Avoid_: Restaurant, cafeteria, store, vendor

### Safety

**Incident**:
A Student's unverified community account of something they observed on a Campus, giving a category, Campus and building or area, a claimed observed time, a short description, and an optional moderator-reviewed photo; it is not a Post and never appears in the Feed.
_Avoid_: Incident report, safety post, community alert, tip

**Incident Update**:
A dated addition beneath an Incident, either an author update or a moderator note; the original Incident is never rewritten.
_Avoid_: Edit, comment, reply, resolution

**Alert**:
A safety notice issued by a named official source (such as KSU Alert, University Police, or KSU emergency management) and shown by KSUX with its source and timestamps; only an official source can create, update, or clear one.
_Avoid_: Community alert, warning, notification

**Moderation Report**:
A Student's request that KSUX Moderators review a piece of content or a Student for a stated reason; it never contacts police.
_Avoid_: Flag, police report, report (unqualified)

**Moderator**:
A volunteer KSUX Student, appointed by maintainers, who reviews Moderation Reports, Incident photos and Appeals and writes moderator notes; not police, not KSU staff, and not on call.
_Avoid_: Admin, dispatcher, officer

**Redaction**:
A Moderator's removal of identifying or prohibited passages from an Incident, always disclosed on the Incident with a moderator note; the only change ever made to an Incident's original text after it is posted.
_Avoid_: Edit, censorship, correction

**Appeal**:
An author's single request, per moderation decision, for a different Moderator to review that decision again.
_Avoid_: Dispute, complaint, ticket

**Emergency Panel**:
The full-screen choice of calling 911, calling KSU Police, or texting KSU Police, shown before every Incident and every Campus Safety Moderation Report.
_Avoid_: SOS button, panic button

### Recognition

**Clout**:
Points a verified Student earns for corroborated, low-stakes logistics contributions such as Parking Reports, Line Checks, Study Room vibes, and completed handoffs; it never measures reliability and is never earned from or shown on safety content.
_Avoid_: Points, karma, reputation, trust score

**Leaderboard**:
The weekly, per-Campus ranking of Students by Clout earned that week, which Students can opt out of appearing on.
_Avoid_: Ranking, hall of fame, scoreboard

### Campus AI

**Scrappy**:
KSUX's campus AI owl assistant able to answer Student questions across all public KSUX modules via a dedicated chat view or `@scrappy` mentions in Feed Posts and replies, without access to Direct Messages, Group Chats, or Deal Chats.
_Avoid_: Bot, copilot, Grok clone
